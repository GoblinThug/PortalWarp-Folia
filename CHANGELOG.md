## [1.0.0] — 2026-10-02

### Added

- Local two-frame warp portals (`/portal warp create`)
- Cross-server single-frame portals with address transfer (`/portal connect create`) for admins
- Frame selection with a **recovery compass** (`RECOVERY_COMPASS`)
- Automatic frame analysis on XY / XZ / YZ planes
- Configurable interior fill material and particle effects
- Commands: help, list, delete, reload
- Permissions: `portalwarp.use`, `portalwarp.admin`
- Portal persistence in `config.yml`
- **Folia** support (`folia-supported: true`)
- Config options for teleport cooldown, max interior size, and particles
